Scientific Grounding & OSDR Datasets
BioVanguard-X is empirically grounded using parameters extracted from 10 peer-reviewed studies within the NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR):
- [OSD-530: JAXA Cell-Free Epigenome Study](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-530)
- [OSD-569: SpaceX Inspiration4 Whole Blood Measurements](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-569)
- [OSD-575: SpaceX Inspiration4 Blood Serum Metabolic & Cytokine Panels](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575)
- [OSD-656: Physiological / Fluid Shift & Biomarker Data Repository](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-656)
- [OSD-816: Spaceflight Multi-Omic / Systemic Stress Response Study](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-816)
- [OSD-897: Neuro-Ocular & Circadian Regulation Biomarkers](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-897)
- [OSD-941: Circadian Rhythm and Sleep Efficiency Spaceflight Study](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-941)
- [OSD-984: Simulated Galactic Cosmic Radiation & Skeletal Muscle Proteomics](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-984)
- [OSD-1018: Social Isolation & Space Radiation Comparative Study](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-1018)
